Be ready

Based on the government's advice on preparing for emergencies (prepare.campaign.gov.uk).

Keep at home

  • Torch, spare batteries and a battery or wind-up radio
  • Charged power banks for phones
  • Bottled water and food that needs no cooking, for a few days
  • Medicines, first aid kit and copies of important documents
  • Some cash, in case card machines are down

If the power goes off

  • Call 105 from any phone to report or get updates on a power cut
  • Unplug appliances and leave one light on so you know when it returns
  • Keep fridge and freezer doors closed
  • Check on neighbours who may need help
  • Join your network's Priority Services Register if anyone at home relies on power for medical needs

Floods and storms

  • Sign up for free flood warnings at gov.uk or call Floodline on 0345 988 1188
  • Move valuables and medicines upstairs when a flood warning is issued
  • Secure bins, trampolines and garden furniture before high winds
  • Never touch fallen power lines; call 105, or 999 if there is danger to life
  • Keep emergency alerts switched on in your phone's settings
